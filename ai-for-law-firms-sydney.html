Confidentiality and privilege

For a law firm, “safe” is not the same as “privileged”. The joint statement on the use of AI in Australian legal practice, published by the Law Society of NSW, the Legal Practice Board of WA and the Victorian Legal Services Board + Commissioner, says lawyers cannot safely enter confidential, sensitive or privileged client information into public AI chatbots, and must verify what AI produces.

We build with that in mind. We work inside the tools your firm already uses, your data stays in your own accounts under your control, and a person checks the work. Read the plain-English version in Privilege Is Not Security. We are not lawyers and this is not legal advice: your own lawyer has the final word on privilege.
