FAQs

Common questions.
Straight answers.

What exactly happens on the first call?+
We come prepared. We've reviewed your intake form and mapped 1–3 specific AI opportunities for your business. On the call, you share your screen, we pick the highest-leverage fix, and we build it together before the call ends. You leave with something running, not a plan.
Do I need to know anything about AI?+
No. You need to know your business. That's the part I can't do without you. The AI part is what I bring. We build it together on your screen so you understand what's running and why, but you don't need a technical background.
Will I need to buy new tools or subscriptions?+
Almost certainly not. Most clients already have everything we need, they're just not using it properly. One of our proof points is zero new subscriptions added. We work with what you've got.
What's the difference between done-with-you and done-for-you?+
Done-with-you means we build together on your screen, you understand what's there and you own it. Done-for-you is available as an upsell, we build independently and hand it over. Most clients start done-with-you and add done-for-you once they see what's possible.
What if I want to cancel?+
Cancel anytime. No lock-in, no minimum term, no penalty. Most clients don't cancel, because by month two the systems are running and the value is visible in the operations hub. But the option is always there.
What if AI doesn't work for my business?+
That's what the assessment is for. If we can't find clear opportunities, we'll say so on the call. The report is yours either way. No wasted money.
Do I need to be technical?+
No. If you can describe how your business runs, I can identify what to automate. You don't touch the tools. I do.
What do I actually get each month on the retainer?+
Two working sessions where we build or optimise something specific. Unlimited async support between sessions. A shared operations hub that tracks every automation and the time it saves. Something ships every cycle.
How is this different from hiring a consultant?+
Most consultants deliver a strategy document after 4-8 weeks. I deliver a working automation on call one. The retainer means I stay embedded in your business, not handing off a deck.
What happens if I want to pause?+
You pause. No questions, no exit fees, no guilt. Pick back up when you're ready. Your operations hub and automations stay yours.
What tools do you use?+
Yours. The whole point is wiring AI into the tools you already pay for. I don't sell software, subscriptions, or platforms. If a new tool is genuinely needed, I'll tell you, but it's never the starting point.
Can I just do the assessment and not continue?+
Absolutely. The assessment is a standalone product. You get a full report with specific recommendations and a 90-day roadmap. Many owners implement the quick wins themselves. The retainer exists for owners who want someone doing the work alongside them.
Is $1,500 a month really enough for all this?+
It is because of how the model works. You're not paying for a team. You're paying for one operator who already built these systems for his own businesses and can configure yours in hours, not weeks. The retainer covers two working sessions, unlimited async, and a shared hub. If a build needs more, done-for-you projects are quoted separately.
