What happens after the audit?+
I review your answers and email you what I find. If there are opportunities worth exploring, I'll suggest the $999 assessment. No pressure, no spam.
Do I have to do the free audit first?+
No. If you already know you want the full assessment, book it directly at book.veruslink.au/assessment. But most people start with the free audit.
Do I need to be technical?+
No. If you can describe how your business runs, I can identify what to automate. You don't touch the tools. I do.
What tools do I need?+
Yours. We wire AI into the tools you already pay for. If something new is genuinely needed I'll tell you, but it's never the starting point.
What does a typical month look like?+
Two working sessions where we build or optimise something specific, plus unlimited async support between them. Something ships every cycle.
How is this different from hiring a consultant?+
Most consultants deliver a strategy document after weeks. I deliver a working automation on call one and stay embedded, rather than handing off a deck.
Why can't I just use ChatGPT myself?+
You can. Most of our clients already have. The difference is applying it to your actual workflows, not just asking it questions. ChatGPT is a tool. VerusLink is the operator who wires it into your invoicing, your follow-ups, your scheduling, and your reporting so it runs without you thinking about it.
What happens if something breaks?+
You message me and I fix it. Usually same business day. Every automation is documented so nothing is a mystery. If I built it, I support it.
What if I want to cancel or pause?+
Cancel or pause anytime. No lock-in, no minimum term, no penalty. Pick back up when you're ready and your automations stay yours.
Is my data safe?+
Yes. We work inside your own tools and accounts, so your data stays with you. We never sell it or move it onto third-party platforms.
