FAQs

Common questions. Straight answers.

What exactly happens on the first call?
We come prepared. We've reviewed your intake form and mapped 1 to 3 specific AI opportunities for your business. On the call, you share your screen, we pick the highest-leverage fix, and we build it together before the call ends. You leave with something running, not a plan.
Do I need to know anything about AI?
No. You need to know your business. That's the part the crew can't do without you. The AI part is what we bring. We build it together on your screen so you understand what's running and why, but you don't need a technical background.
Will I need to buy new tools or subscriptions?
Almost certainly not. Most clients already have everything we need, they're just not using it properly. One of our proof points is zero new subscriptions added. Forge works with what you've got.
What's the difference between done-with-you and done-for-you?
Done-with-you means we build together on your screen, you understand what's there and you own it. Done-for-you is available as an upsell, we build independently and hand it over. Most clients start done-with-you and add done-for-you once they see what's possible.
What if I want to cancel?
Cancel anytime. No lock-in, no minimum term, no penalty. Most clients don't cancel, because by month two the systems are running and the value is visible in the Notion hub. But the option is always there.
