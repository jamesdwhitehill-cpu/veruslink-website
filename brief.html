Your Business Brief

Nine questions. A map you keep forever.

Most people get beige answers out of AI because they hand it a blank box and no context. This fixes that. Answer nine plain questions about your business the way you would tell a mate, and you end up with the context any AI tool needs to actually understand your world.

Download the interview (PDF) Two pages. Print it, or fill it in on screen.

How it works

Step 01

Download the sheet and answer nine plain questions in your own words.

Step 02

What you write is your Business Brief — one page that says how the place really runs.

Step 03

Paste it at the top of any AI chat. It finally gets your business.

The nine questions

Nothing here is a trick. They are the nine things anyone would need to know before they could be useful to you.

  1. 01What does your business do, and who do you do it for?
  2. 02What is your role, and how many people are on the team?
  3. 03Walk through a normal week. Where does your time actually go?
  4. 04What is the one job you dread or keep putting off?
  5. 05How do you win work, and who are your best kind of customers?
  6. 06What are your rules? The way you do things a new hire would get wrong.
  7. 07What tools or software do you already run the business on?
  8. 08Where have you tried AI before, and where did it not stick?
  9. 09If you got one day a week back, what would you do with it?
