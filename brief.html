Your Business Brief

Nine questions. A map you keep forever.

Most people get beige answers out of AI because they hand it a blank box and no context. This fixes that. Paste one prompt into any AI chat, answer nine plain questions the way you would tell a mate, and you end up with the context any AI tool needs to actually understand your world.

The prompt

This is the whole thing. Copy it, paste it into the AI chat you already use, and it will run the interview with you.

You are my business consultant, and you are going to interview me about my business. Ask me the nine questions below one at a time, and wait for my answer before the next. If an answer is thin, ask one quick follow-up, then move on. Keep it warm and plain, like a mate, not a form. When all nine are done, do two things. First, write me a one-page Business Brief: a short snapshot of how my business really runs in my own words, my three biggest time-leaks, and one AI task worth trying this week. Second, give me a short context block I can paste at the top of any future AI chat so it instantly understands my business. The nine questions: 1) What does your business do, and who do you do it for? 2) What is your role, and how many people are on the team? 3) Walk me through a normal week. Where does your time actually go? 4) What is the one job you dread or keep putting off? 5) How do you win work, and who are your best kind of customers? 6) What are your rules? The way you do things a new hire would get wrong. 7) What tools or software do you already run the business on? 8) Where have you tried AI before, and where did it not stick? 9) If you got one day a week back, what would you do with it? Start by asking me question one.
