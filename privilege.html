The Privilege-Safe AI Rule

One page. The simple test for what can and can never go near AI in a law firm. Pin it above every desk.

The three tiers
Never

Named client matters. Privileged advice. This never goes into any public AI tool. Full stop. Not for a draft, not for a summary, not to save five minutes. The privilege attached to this work does not survive contact with a consumer AI system.

Behind Glass

The same work, inside a private system that does not train on your data. A properly configured firm-grade AI environment. This is where the time savings are. Getting here takes setup - but it is where AI earns its place in a law firm.

Open

Generic, non-identifying work. Templates, precedents with no client details, general research summaries, admin drafts. Use freely. If removing the client name makes it safe, it belongs here.

In United States v. Heppner (US Federal Court, 17 February 2026) a party lost privilege after putting case details into a public consumer AI tool. This card keeps your firm on the right side of that line.
