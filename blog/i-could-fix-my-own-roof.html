I Could Fix My Own Roof

30 June 2026 · 2 min read

I could fix my own roof.

The knowledge is right there. A weekend of videos and I would know which tiles, which sealant, how to flash around the chimney. None of it is secret. All of it is free.

I still call a roofer. Every time.

Not because I could not learn it. Because I have a business to run, a family to see, and I am not going to spend my Saturday on a ladder to save a few hundred dollars and risk my neck.

That gap is the whole point.

Knowledge free, time not

Every service that has ever existed works this way.

You can read every law online. Lawyers are busy. You can watch surgery on the internet. Surgeons are booked out. The recipes are all free. Restaurants are full.

Information being available has never once killed demand for the people who apply it. What people are short on has never been knowledge. It has been time, and the bandwidth to act on what they know.

The roofer is not selling a secret. He is selling the fact that you do not have to think about your roof. You call, it gets handled, you get on with your life.

AI does not break this. It proves it.

People look at AI and assume it ends services like ours. If anyone can ask a chatbot how to automate their invoices, why hire someone to do it?

Same reason you do not fix your own roof.

The knowledge was already free before AI. AI just makes it faster to find. It does not give you the hours to build the thing, run it, fix it when it breaks, and keep it running while you do your actual job.

If anything, the better the tools get, the more valuable the person who applies them becomes. More power means more that could be done, and the same twenty-four hours to do it in. The bottleneck moves further onto time, which is exactly where we sit.

Where we fit

We are the roofer for your AI.

You do not need to learn the tools. You do not need to wire them together. You do not need to babysit them at 11pm when something breaks.

You call. It gets handled. You get on with the business.

The knowledge was never the hard part. Doing it, for you, while you do everything else, is the job. That is what we sell. And AI getting better only makes that job worth more.
