06 / FAQs
What happens after the Health Check?+

Your results land in your inbox. If there’s real opportunity, we’ll suggest the $999 + GST assessment. If there isn’t, we’ll tell you straight.

Do I have to do the free Health Check first?+

No. You can book the assessment directly if you already know you want it. Most people start with the Health Check.

Do I need to be technical?+

No. You describe how your business runs. We handle the tools, the wiring and the jargon.

What tools do I need?+

The ones you already have. New subscriptions are rarely needed. We build inside your existing stack.

What does a typical month look like?+

We run one job for you, with a person checking the work. Two working sessions a month to check in and pick the next job, plus unlimited async support in between. Something ships every cycle.

How is this different from hiring a consultant?+

Consultants leave you a strategy deck after weeks of discovery. We leave you working systems, starting day one.

Why can’t I just use ChatGPT myself?+

You can. The gap is wiring it into your actual workflows so it runs without you thinking about it. That’s the job.

What happens if something breaks?+

I fix it. Usually the same business day. You message the founder, not a support queue.

What if I want to cancel or pause?+

No lock-in. Cancel or pause with 14 days notice.

Is my data safe?+

It stays in your own accounts, under your control. Never sold, never moved, never used to train anything.
